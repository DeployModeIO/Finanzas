# LinkedIn Post - Meridiano ETF Analyzer

## Post Copy (English)

🚀 Just launched: Meridiano — a free, open-source PWA for simulating and tracking global ETF investments.

Built as a "patient investor's ledger," it brings together:

📊 Portfolio simulation with KPIs, P&L, and allocation tracking
🌍 40 global ETFs with technical analysis (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands)
📈 Mean-variance optimization with risk profiles
🤖 Educational ML models: Monte Carlo, Q-learning, sentiment analysis
📱 Offline-first PWA — works on desktop and mobile
🌙 Real light/dark themes

⚠️ Important: This is an **educational simulation tool**, not for real investing. Practice risk management, test strategies, and prepare decisions — but never trade real money here.

👉 Try it live: https://finanzas-eta-amber.vercel.app/
📦 Open source: https://github.com/DeployModeIO/Finanzas
